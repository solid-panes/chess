Chess pane

A chess game kept in a Solid pod, played in the SolidOS data browser. The pane is one module, loaded by link.

Open the Opera Game

The data browser asks before it adds a pane from a site it has not seen. Allow it, and the game opens on a board: play on against a person or against Stockfish.

The pane: https://solid-panes.github.io/chess/chess-pane.js

Source and details: github.com/solid-panes/chess
